## Logo & Visual Identity

- **Logo**: a multicolour diamond kite (cyan / orange / magenta / green facets) flying on a blue sky with clouds; kite string decorated with small bows. Bangla wordmark "ইচ্ছে ঘুড়ি" in a warm red/rainbow gradient; green tagline "আবহমান বাংলার ঐতিহ্য" beneath.
- **Mood**: joyful, colourful, nostalgic Bengali heritage (kite = childhood wishes/dreams) combined with an "elegance" line for accessories.
- **Marketing creatives**: warm, cozy, soft-light photography (satin scrunchies at night with fairy-light bokeh; jute bag on a riverside picnic table). Watermark style: logo bottom corner + phone number.
- Asset files: `images/facebook/` (this repo) — `profile-logo.jpg` is the master logo.
